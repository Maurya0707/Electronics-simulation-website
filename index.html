Simulink X Studyhub - Complete User Simulation Manual

1. Introduction to the Virtual Laboratory

Simulink X Studyhub is an interactive electronics engineering simulator designed specifically for undergraduate B.Tech laboratory courses. Every experiment accurately computes physical circuit behaviors based on standard semiconductor physics, differential equations, and Kirchhoff's circuit laws.

2. Step-by-Step Procedure to Run Any Experiment

  • Select Experiment: Use the top-right dropdown menu to pick any of the 8 B.Tech experiments (Ohm's Law, Diode, Rectifier, BJT, RC Filter, Op-Amp, Logic Gates, 555 Timer).
  • Configure Parameters: Adjust circuit controls on the right panel (Voltage sliders, Resistor values, Capacitor filters, Bias switches).
  • Switch Workbench Views: Click Circuit Schematic to observe dynamic particle current flow; click Dual-Trace DSO for oscilloscope waveforms; click X-Y Plotter for scientific graphs.
  • Log Observations: Click "Add Reading to Table" to save the current experimental reading into the Observation Table.
  • Plot & Analyze: Click "Plot..." to immediately display the characteristic curve with auto-calculated regression lines and slopes.
  • Export & Report: Download readings as a standard .csv file or click "Lab Report" to generate and print your official graded record sheet.

3. Operating the Dual-Trace DSO (Oscilloscope)

  • Timebase: Adjust the time per division (0.5 ms to 20 ms/div) to spread or condense waveforms horizontally.
  • Volts/Div: Scale Channel 1 (Yellow, Input) and Channel 2 (Cyan, Output) vertically for optimum observation.
  • Run / Stop: Freeze live waveforms at any instant to inspect phase shifts, peak voltages, and harmonic clipping.
  • On-Screen Readouts (OSD): Automated measurements of \(V_{pp}\), \(V_{rms}\), and timebase are displayed directly at the top and bottom of the CRT screen.

4. Using the Digital Multimeter (DMM)

  • Select measurement mode: DC V (DC Voltage), AC V (AC RMS Voltage), DC mA (Current), Ω (Resistance), or Diode.
  • Click HOLD to lock the active reading on the LCD.

5. Viva Voce & Knowledge Self-Assessment

Switch to the Viva Voce & Conceptual Quiz tab at the top. Answer the 5 curated questions for your active experiment, click submit, and receive your instant score along with detailed technical explanations designed to prepare you for laboratory oral examinations.
